## 🚀 HOW TO CREATE YOUR OWN LAUNCHER

1. Open Shrapnel's Launcher Builder.
2. Enter your server or community name.
3. Choose your custom PNG banner.
4. Select your executable ICO icon.
5. Enter an optional mod download URL.
6. Preview your banner using Fit or Fill mode.
7. Choose where to save your finished launcher.
8. Click **BUILD CUSTOM EXE**.
9. Wait for the build to complete.
10. Your personalized Windows launcher is ready to share with your community!
